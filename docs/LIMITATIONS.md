# Interpretation limits

This is an author-implemented software evaluation, not third-party replication, independent instrumentation, a calibrated industrial plant or physical PLC/HMI validation. The two-tank plant is separately implemented but shares the approved-target requirement and original HMAC verifier. Tank areas, outflow coefficients and PI gains are fixed. Local PI feedback is ideal; Gaussian noise applies only to remote observations. Network delay and clocks in the two-tank campaign are virtual.

Authentication rejects the tested modification and replay packets but cannot establish that a signer is honest or a genuine sensor is accurate. Two authenticated W=8 declarations after manipulation ends remain premature because genuine measurements lag a boundary crossing. Longer windows can wait out an attack or move the evaluation interval, and can withhold declarations that were not premature at shorter windows.

The 34.16-percentage-point premature-outcome improvement is conditional on the 1,768 traces where G3 and G8+A both declare. It is not an all-trace deployment effect. The twenty seed blocks preserve every crossed factor cell. Conditional bootstrap intervals do not measure industrial incident prevalence, new fault families, physical-parameter uncertainty or hardware uncertainty.

This release reproduces recorded evidence and computational findings. It does not prove novelty, review acceptance, operational enforcement, reduced physical harm or a universally optimal window.
